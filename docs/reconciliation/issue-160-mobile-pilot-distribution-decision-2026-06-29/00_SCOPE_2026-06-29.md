## Scope

This is a **docs-only** decision checklist. It consolidates the merged iOS and
Android distribution preflight evidence into a single GO / NO-GO record for the
mobile pilot distribution decision. It introduces **no code, config, signing,
deploy, or store changes**.

## What this checklist did NOT do (non-negotiable)

- No build upload (TestFlight / App Store / Google Play).
- No signing/certificate/profile/keystore changes.
- No deployment, no migrations, no live Supabase data touched, no env changes.
- No secrets printed or committed (no `.p8`, `.env`, `.xcconfig` values, keystores,
  service account JSON, tokens, APK/AAB/xcarchive artifacts).
- No iOS or Android source changes.
- No pilot-live, store-readiness, or production-GA claim.

## Decision policy

Readiness verdicts are sourced strictly from the merged #161/#162 evidence and use
`READY` / `BLOCKED` / `OWNER_ACTION_REQUIRED` and a final `GO` / `NO-GO`.
