## 1. What AISTROYKA will do during the pilot

AISTROYKA runs a **controlled pilot** (not a public product launch) for **[Client company / TBD]** on project **[Project name / TBD]**:

- Assign work to field workers
- Collect **photo reports** from site
- Let managers **review and approve** submissions
- Give stakeholders a **safe view** of progress (no internal contractor finances)

We provide onboarding, support during business hours, and a **Day 5** check-in. Platform: **web dashboard** + **iOS apps via TestFlight**.

---

## 2. What workers do daily

1. Open **AISTROYKA Worker** on **iPhone** (TestFlight).
2. Sign in → **start shift**.
3. Open assigned **tasks**.
4. Create a **report** with **before/after photos** and a short note.
5. **Submit**; resubmit if manager requests changes.

Weak connectivity: finish when back online; app syncs when network returns.

---

## 3. What managers do daily

1. Sign in to **web dashboard** (primary); optional **Manager** iOS app.
2. Review **approvals / reports inbox**.
3. **Approve**, **reject**, or **request changes** with a note.
4. Assign tasks for the next day.
5. Optional: internal documents and cost tracking (**not visible to client portal**).

Target: review within **24–48 hours**.

---

## 4. What the client / stakeholder can see

Portal access (if invited):

- Project progress and status
- Approved reports and shared photos
- Client-facing documents and decisions

**Not visible:** internal costs, margins, subcontractor costs, internal budget pressure.
