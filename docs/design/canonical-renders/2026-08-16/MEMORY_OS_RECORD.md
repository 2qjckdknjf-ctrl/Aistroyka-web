# AISTROYKA — Canonical Final Redesign Memory Record

**Status:** FINAL / APPROVED CANONICAL CONCEPT  
**Approved:** 2026-08-16  
**Scope:** product design, responsive IA, Liquid Glass visual system, motion language, implementation reference

## Canonical decision

The final AISTROYKA redesign approved on 2026-08-16 is the visual and architectural source of truth for future product-design and implementation work. New work must evolve the existing AISTROYKA product rather than reinterpret it as a different product.

## Canonical surfaces

1. Dashboard
2. Projects
3. Project Command Center
4. Tasks
5. Reports & Review
6. Documents & Drawings
7. Schedule & Milestones
8. Team & Contractors
9. AI Risks & Analytics
10. Owner / Client Portal

Every surface must be designed as one coherent system across **desktop, tablet and phone**.

## Visual direction

- Premium AI construction operating system.
- Deep graphite / near-black foundation.
- AISTROYKA construction yellow remains the main brand/action accent.
- Liquid Glass is a core material: transparency, blur, refraction, tint, sheen, layered depth and soft edge highlights.
- Controlled cyan / electric blue / violet spectral lighting is reserved for AI, intelligence and advanced system states.
- Semantic green / orange / red are used for success, watch and risk states.
- Interfaces should feel alive through restrained micro-motion, live widgets, reactive buttons and meaningful state transitions rather than decorative animation noise.

## Architecture principles

- Reduce administrative-dashboard density and visual noise.
- Put decision-making, risk, current work and required action ahead of secondary navigation.
- Use one understandable primary navigation model; avoid duplicate/competing tab systems.
- Project Command Center is the central operational workspace.
- Mobile is field-first: fast review, capture, approve/reject/request changes, tasks, photos and essential project context.
- Tablet balances field operation with management overview.
- Desktop provides portfolio, analytics, planning and high-density management workflows.
- Owner / Client Portal is intentionally simplified and must not expose contractor/admin controls.

## Motion principles

- Glass materialization/reveal on entry where useful.
- Pointer/hover depth for signature cards only.
- Reactive sheen/glow follows interaction subtly.
- Primary buttons have tactile press, hover lift and controlled light response.
- Live data widgets animate only when state/data changes.
- AI states may use restrained spectral pulse/drift.
- Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and preserve accessibility.

## Product boundary

AISTROYKA is an **AI construction management platform**. The canonical redesign must never turn the product into a building-material store, generic marketplace, catalogue or estimator-only application.

## Implementation rule

All future Cursor/agent design implementation should use this record and the accompanying canonical render pack as the source of truth. Preserve existing business logic, RBAC, tenant isolation, backend contracts and production-critical flows while evolving the UI/UX.
